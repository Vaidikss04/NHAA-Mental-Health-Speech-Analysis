# NHAA AI Assist

## AI-Based Real-Time Stress and Trauma Assessment Module for Victims/Complainants Accessing NHAA (14566) and Integrated Portal

---

## Problem Statement

Victims and complainants accessing the NHAA (14566) helpline and Integrated Portal may experience high levels of stress, fear, anxiety, and emotional distress while communicating their concerns.

This project presents an AI-assisted speech analysis prototype intended to help human operators identify emotional and stress-related indicators from speech and support prioritization of cases requiring attention.

---

## Project Overview

NHAA AI Assist is an AI-based speech analysis prototype adapted for the NHAA 14566 and Integrated Portal use case.

The system accepts speech input and performs AI-based analysis to identify emotional and mental-health-related indicators from the provided audio.

The system is intended to assist human operators and does not replace professional or clinical assessment.
